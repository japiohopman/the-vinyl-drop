# The Vinyl Drop

**Buy. Trade. Dig.**

The Vinyl Drop is a local community marketplace for people who love physical records — crate diggers, collectors, DJs and anyone who gets a little thrill from finding the right pressing.

Rooted in the spirit of Amsterdam Oost, it is built around real records and the people who own them. Browse what is available, discover a specific copy, ask the seller a question, and arrange a local buy or trade.

**[Explore The Vinyl Drop](https://thevinyldrop.nl)**

Account creation remains invite-only while the private beta and public-launch safeguards are completed. Public pages can be explored without an account; access to seller and member actions remains controlled.

## Made for people who love digging

- **Find your next record.** Search by artist, album title, label or catalogue number. Narrow results by genre, condition, price and trade availability.
- **Know the copy, not just the album.** Every listing describes a specific physical record, with media and sleeve condition, photos, notes and release details.
- **Meet the person behind the record.** Public seller profiles add context, while listing comments let buyers and sellers ask questions.
- **Buy, sell or trade locally.** Share a record from your collection and connect with someone who wants to give it a new home.

## A marketplace, not a mystery box

Records are more than a title and a cover image. The Vinyl Drop puts the details that matter to collectors up front — from pressing information to condition — while keeping the experience personal and community-minded.

Transactions are arranged directly between buyers and sellers. Built-in checkout, payment processing, escrow and platform-arranged shipping are not part of the current experience.
