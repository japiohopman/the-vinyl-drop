# The Vinyl Drop

**Buy. Trade. Dig.**

The Vinyl Drop is a local marketplace for people who love physical records — crate diggers, collectors, and anyone who gets a little thrill from finding the right pressing.

Rooted in the spirit of Amsterdam Oost, it is built around real records and the people who own them. Browse what is out there, discover a copy, ask the seller a question, and arrange a local buy or trade.

**🎧 [Check out The Vinyl Drop](https://the-vinyl-drop-staging.onrender.com)**  
*Explore the current staging preview — the site is still a work in progress.*

## Made for people who love digging

- **Find your next record.** Search by artist, album title, label, or catalogue number. Narrow the results by genre, condition, price, and whether the seller is open to a trade.
- **Know the copy, not just the album.** Listings describe a specific physical record, with media and sleeve condition, photos, notes, and release details.
- **Meet the person behind the record.** Public seller profiles add a little context, while listing comments give buyers and sellers a place to ask questions.
- **Buy, sell, or trade locally.** Share a record from your collection and connect with someone who wants to give it a new home.

## A marketplace, not a mystery box

Records are more than a title and a cover image. The Vinyl Drop puts the details that matter to collectors up front — from pressing information to condition — while keeping the experience personal and community-minded.

Transactions are arranged directly between buyers and sellers. Built-in checkout, shipping, and payment processing are not part of the current experience.
